What Makes This Different

Policy-Level Detail

No vague axes or abstract scores. Every answer maps to concrete policies — you'll see exactly what you support on taxation, property rights, free speech, immigration, and dozens more topics.

Multi-Option Questions

Many questions allow you to select multiple answers, capturing the nuance of real political views. Some options are mutually exclusive — the quiz handles this automatically.

Save & Compare

Save your results locally and come back anytime. Take the quiz again later to see how your views evolved, or compare your profile with a friend's side by side.

Shareable Image

Your results are rendered into a compact, newspaper-styled image you can download and share — a four-column summary of your entire ideological profile.
